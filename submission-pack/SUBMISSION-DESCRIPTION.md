BuyerShield Desk is a project built on top of Buyer Protection Oracle for buyer
protection workflows in community marketplaces and escrow-driven commerce.

The app lets an operator create a claim from public policy, product, and
evidence URLs, attach a merchant response, resolve the case through a live
GenLayer intelligent contract, and read the final verdict back into an action
panel that can guide refund release, payout blocking, or escalation.

This makes GenLayer central to the project workflow rather than decorative.
